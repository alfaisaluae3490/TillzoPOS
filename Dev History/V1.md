## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Over the development iterations leading to **V1**, TillzoPOS was elevated from a basic local POS into an **Enterprise-Grade, Multi-Country, Offline-First Point of Sale & Business Intelligence Platform**.

Key milestones achieved:
- **Zero Data Loss on Reinstall / Multi-Device**: Auto-discovery and clean linking of Google Drive folders and Spreadsheets.
- **Global Tax Compliance**: 11 country presets (USA, UK, PK, IN, SA, AE, CA, DE, AU, CN, etc.) with ZATCA Phase 2 TLV QR support.
- **Vendor Accounts Payable (AP)**: 100% Credit, Partial Payments, Debit Notes, Due Date tracking, and Daily WorkManager Reminders.
- **Vector PDF Invoicing**: High-resolution A4 / Letter PDF invoices with full business branding, itemized taxes, WhatsApp document sharing, and native Android `PrintManager` support.
- **Visual Business Analytics**: Ultra-clear dashboard displaying Net Profit & Loss, COGS, visual revenue charts, category rankings, product performance, dead stock alerts, and customer retention metrics.

## 2. MODULE 1: MULTI-STORE PROVISIONING & SHEET/FOLDER PERSISTENCE FIXES

### Problem Statement:
- Reinstalling the app or signing in on a secondary device created duplicate folders/sheets instead of linking the existing store folder and spreadsheet.
- Sheet selection UI did not properly surface existing sheets or newly created business folders.

### Technical Implementation:
1. **Google Drive API Provisioning Pipeline**:
   - Fixed `SheetPickerViewModel.kt` and `SheetPickerScreen.kt` to perform deterministic folder queries (`mimeType = 'application/vnd.google-apps.folder'` and `trashed = false`).
   - Implemented persistent storage in `AppSetupPrefs.kt` (`KEY_SPREADSHEET_ID`, `KEY_BUSINESS_FOLDER_ID`, `KEY_GRN_FOLDER_ID`).
2. **Policy Compliance**:
   - Adhered 100% to Google Play Store & Google Identity / Drive API Scopes policies (restricted to `drive.file` scope).
3. **Crash Guards**:
   - Guarded against null metadata during first-time onboarding initialization.

## 3. MODULE 2: CLEAN DECOUPLING OF LEGACY ADMIN DASHBOARD / USER MANAGEMENT

### Problem Statement:
- The merchant requested removing the complex user management and legacy admin dashboard to simplify cashier operation without corrupting POS checkout, till sessions, or Google Sheets sync.

### Technical Implementation:
1. **Clean Route Removal**:
   - Safely decoupled orphaned user management screens from navigation graphs in `AppNavHost.kt`.
2. **Preserving Sync Integrity**:
   - Retained the `cashier_id` attribute on `SaleEntity` and `TillSessionEntity` using the active session/owner profile, preventing schema breaks in Google Sheets.
3. **Security & Session Lock**:
   - Preserved `AdminPasscode` and `MasterKey` in `AppSetupPrefs.kt` for locking sensitive actions (Settings, Local DB Reset, Refunds).

## 4. MODULE 3: GLOBAL MULTI-COUNTRY TAX ENGINE (11 REGIONAL PRESETS & ZATCA QR)

### Problem Statement:
- The app needed to launch globally across diverse tax regimes (USA, Pakistan, India, UK, Germany, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Canada, Australia, China) with both Tax Inclusive and Tax Exclusive rules, dynamic tax IDs (TRN, NTN, GSTIN, VAT), and legal e-invoice QR codes.

### Technical Implementation:
1. **`TaxUtils.kt` Engine**:
   - Defined presets for 11 countries:
     - **SA (Saudi Arabia)**: VAT 15%, ZATCA Phase 2 TLV Base64 QR Code, TRN ID.
     - **AE (UAE)**: VAT 5%, TRN ID.
     - **PK (Pakistan)**: Sales Tax 18%, NTN / STRN ID.
     - **IN (India)**: GST 18%, GSTIN ID.
     - **US (USA)**: Sales Tax 8.25% (Exclusive), State Tax ID / EIN.
     - **UK (United Kingdom)**: VAT 20% (Inclusive), UK VAT ID.
     - **DE (Germany)**: MwSt 19% (Inclusive), USt-IdNr.
     - **CA (Canada)**: HST / GST 13%, Business Number (BN).
     - **AU (Australia)**: GST 10%, ABN.
     - **CN (China)**: VAT 13%, Unified Social Credit Code.
2. **ZATCA TLV Base64 Encoding**:
   - Implemented Tag-Length-Value (TLV) binary encoder for tags 1-5 (Seller Name, VAT Registration Number, ISO-8601 Timestamp, Total with VAT, VAT Total).
3. **App Setup Preferences Integration**:
   - Stored in `AppSetupPrefs.kt`: `countryCode`, `taxNumber`, `taxLabel`, `defaultTaxRate`, `taxInclusive`, `enableZatcaQr`.

## 7. MODULE 6: PROFESSIONAL VECTOR PDF TAX INVOICE ENGINE & RECEIPT UPGRADE

### Problem Statement:
- Receipts were only shared as raw plain text without proper formatting, business details, tax breakdowns, or PDF generation.

### Technical Implementation:
1. **`InvoicePdfGenerator.kt` (`com.tillzo.pos.utils.pdf`)**:
   - High-resolution vector PDF generator using native Android `android.graphics.pdf.PdfDocument`.
   - **Header**: Business Name, Full Address, Phone/WhatsApp, Website, Email/Socials, Tax/TRN Registration Number, and `TAX INVOICE` / `SIMPLIFIED TAX INVOICE` badge.
   - **Metadata Box**: Invoice #, Date & Time, Cashier Name, POS Terminal ID, Customer Profile (Name, Phone, Address, Email).
   - **Itemized Table**: `#`, `ITEM / DESCRIPTION`, `QTY`, `UNIT PRICE`, `TAX %`, `TAX AMT`, `TOTAL`.
   - **Financial Summary**: Gross Subtotal, Line & Order Discounts, Tax Breakdown by Rate, and Grand Total (Net Payable).
   - **Settlement Details**: Multi-tender breakdown (Cash, Card, Wallet, Credit, Change Returned).
   - **Embedded QR Code**: ZATCA Phase 2 TLV / Digital verification QR rendered onto canvas.
   - **Action Methods**:
     - `generateInvoicePdf()`: Saves PDF file to cache directory.
     - `openPdf()`: Opens invoice in device PDF viewer via `FileProvider`.
     - `sharePdfOnWhatsApp()`: Directly attaches the `.pdf` invoice document to WhatsApp chat.
     - `sharePdfGeneral()`: General share sheet (Email, Drive, Bluetooth).
     - `printPdf()`: Prints via Android's native `PrintManager` with `PrintDocumentAdapter`.
2. **`ReceiptScreen.kt` UI Upgrade**:
   - Added **"View PDF"** button.

## 8. MODULE 7: VISUAL BUSINESS ANALYTICS & INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD

### Problem Statement:
- Business owners needed an intuitive, visual, and comprehensive analytics dashboard to track Profit & Loss, sales trends, category performance, dead stock, expenses, and customer loyalty.

### Technical Implementation:
1. **`AnalyticsViewModel.kt` (`com.tillzo.pos.ui.analytics`)**:
   - **Time Filters**: `TODAY`, `YESTERDAY`, `THIS_WEEK`, `THIS_MONTH`, `LAST_MONTH`, `CUSTOM`.
   - **P&L Engine**:
     - `Gross Sales = Sum(Sale.total)`
     - `COGS = Sum(Item.quantity * Item.cost_price)`
     - `Gross Profit = Gross Sales - COGS`
     - `Gross Margin % = (Gross Profit / Gross Sales) * 100`
     - `Operating Expenses = Sum(Expense.amount)`
     - `Wastage Losses = Sum(Wastage.totalLoss)`
     - `Net Profit = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses - Wastage Losses`
     - `Net Margin % = (Net Profit / Gross Sales) * 100`
     - `AOV = Gross Sales / Total Orders`
   - **Rankings & Distributions**:
     - Category revenue, profit, units sold, and % share.
     - Top 10 best-selling products.
     - Slow-moving / 0-sale products alert.
     - Cashflow distribution (Cash, Card, Wallet, Khata/Credit).
     - Store inventory valuation at Cost vs Retail & potential profit.
     - Customer loyalty & repeat visit rate %.
2. **`AnalyticsScreen.kt` (`com.tillzo.pos.ui.analytics`)**:
   - **Hero Profit Banner**: Glowing Emerald Green for Net Profit / Crimson Red for Net Loss with large bold amount and margin badge.
   - **4-Box Metric Grid**: Gross Sales, COGS, Operating Expenses, Wastage Losses.
   - **Visual Trend Chart**: Pure Compose & Canvas vector bar chart.
   - **Tender Distribution**: Multi-segment progress bar.
   - **Category Performance**: Visual progress bars per category.
   - **Top Products Card**: Ranked with Gold 🥇, Silver 🥈, Bronze 🥉 badges.
   - **Custom Date Range Picker**: Material 3 `DateRangePicker` modal.
3. **Navigation Integration**:
   - Added shortcut icon in `HomeScreen.kt` TopAppBar (`AutoGraph` icon).
   - Added "Business Analytics & Insights" in `AdvancedMenuSheet.kt`.
   - Added route `composable("analytics_screen")` in `AppNavHost.kt`.
